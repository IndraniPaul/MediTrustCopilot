# DEMONSTRATION-ONLY Synthetic SOP: Evidence Request Triage Queue

## Disclaimer
This is synthetic demonstration-only hospital-process content for the MediTrust AI MVP. It is not approved clinical policy and must not be used for patient care decisions.

## Workflow
Evidence requests are triaged by topic, urgency, source type, and requester role. Clinical advice, diagnosis, treatment selection, or dosage requests are routed for human review instead of being answered by the knowledge service.
