# WHO Guidelines on Hand Hygiene in Health Care

## Overview
WHO hand hygiene guidance supports healthcare workers, administrators, and health authorities with evidence-informed recommendations to reduce transmission of pathogenic microorganisms in healthcare.

## Indications
Hand hygiene should be performed at moments that protect patients and healthcare personnel, including before touching a patient, before clean or aseptic procedures, after body fluid exposure risk, after touching a patient, and after touching patient surroundings.

## Implementation
Successful hand hygiene improvement depends on system change, training, evaluation, reminders in the workplace, and an institutional safety climate.
