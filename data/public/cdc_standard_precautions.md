# CDC Standard Precautions for All Patient Care

## Recommendation Details
Standard Precautions use common-sense practices and personal protective equipment to protect healthcare providers and prevent spread from patient to patient. They are used for all patient care and are based on risk assessment.

## Respiratory Hygiene
Respiratory hygiene and cough etiquette include source control measures, patient education, and placement considerations. Facilities should make respiratory hygiene materials available and encourage covering coughs and performing hand hygiene.

## Patient Placement
Patient placement should consider infection transmission risk. Infection-control guidance should be followed when special precautions are needed.
