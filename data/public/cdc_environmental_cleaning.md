# CDC Environmental Cleaning and Disinfection Basics

## Environmental Cleaning
Healthcare settings should clean and disinfect environmental surfaces according to risk, frequency of touch, and manufacturer instructions. High-touch surfaces require consistent attention because they can contribute to transmission.

## Reusable Equipment
Reusable medical equipment should be reprocessed between each patient or when soiled. Cleaning removes material before disinfection or sterilization steps.

## Supplies
Facilities should make cleaning supplies and instructions available to staff responsible for environmental services and patient-care equipment.
