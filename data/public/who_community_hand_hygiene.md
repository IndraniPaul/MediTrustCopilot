# WHO Guidelines on Hand Hygiene in Community Settings

## Scope
This guidance concerns hand hygiene in non-healthcare community settings. It focuses on domestic, public, and institutional settings where healthcare is not routinely delivered.

## Outcomes
Hand hygiene in community settings can reduce diarrhoeal diseases, acute respiratory infections, and other preventable diseases.

## Boundary
This source is not a hospital clinical policy and should be filtered carefully when a user asks specifically for healthcare worker practices.
