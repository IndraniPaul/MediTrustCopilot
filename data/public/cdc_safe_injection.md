# CDC Safe Injection Practices

## Safe Injection
Safe injection practices are part of Standard Precautions. Needles and syringes should be used safely, single-dose items should not be reused inappropriately, and aseptic technique should be maintained.

## Sharps
Sharps should be handled carefully and disposed of in approved sharps containers. Staff should avoid recapping needles except where policy and safety-engineered procedures require it.

## Medication Preparation
Medication preparation areas should be clean and separated from contaminated items. This document does not provide dosage or prescribing guidance.
