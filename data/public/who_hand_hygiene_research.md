# WHO Research for Hand Hygiene in Health Care 2023-2030

## Research Agenda
Hand hygiene is vital for safe healthcare delivery, and practices at the point of care remain suboptimal worldwide. A research agenda helps identify factors influencing behavior and interventions that strengthen improvement.

## Stakeholders
The agenda supports researchers, decision-makers, donors, and national stakeholders who plan hand hygiene promotion programs.

## Measurement
Measurement can include compliance observation, infrastructure availability, product use, training uptake, and healthcare-associated infection indicators.
