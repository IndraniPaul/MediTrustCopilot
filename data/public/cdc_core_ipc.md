# CDC Core Infection Prevention and Control Practices

## Standard Precautions
Standard Precautions are basic infection prevention practices that apply to all patient care. They include hand hygiene, personal protective equipment when exposure is anticipated, respiratory hygiene, safe injection practices, cleaning and disinfection of equipment and environmental surfaces, and safe handling of textiles and laundry.

## Hand Hygiene
Healthcare personnel should perform hand hygiene before touching a patient, before aseptic tasks, after body fluid exposure risk, after touching a patient or patient environment, and immediately after glove removal. Alcohol-based hand rub is preferred in many clinical situations when hands are not visibly soiled. Soap and water should be used when hands are visibly soiled.

## PPE
Personal protective equipment should be selected based on the anticipated interaction and likely exposure to blood, body fluids, mucous membranes, non-intact skin, or contaminated surfaces. Gloves, gowns, masks, eye protection, and face shields are used according to expected exposure.
