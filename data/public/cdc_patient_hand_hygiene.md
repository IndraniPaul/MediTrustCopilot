# CDC Hand Hygiene for Patients in Healthcare Settings

## Patient Role
Patients and loved ones can help prevent spread of germs by cleaning hands and reminding healthcare personnel to clean hands.

## Facts
Hands can spread germs in healthcare settings. Alcohol-based sanitizer kills many germs quickly, and soap and water remains important when hands are visibly dirty.

## Education
Patient-facing education should be plain language and should not replace instructions from healthcare personnel.
