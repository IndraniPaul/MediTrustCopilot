# CDC Clinical Safety: Hand Hygiene for Healthcare Workers

## Key Points
Hand hygiene protects healthcare personnel and patients. It includes handwashing with soap and water, antiseptic hand rub, and surgical hand antisepsis. Cleaning hands reduces spread of germs to patients and lowers healthcare personnel exposure.

## When to Clean Hands
Healthcare personnel may need to clean hands frequently during a shift. Hand hygiene is important before patient contact, before clean tasks, after possible exposure to body fluids, after touching the patient environment, and after removing gloves.

## Skin Health
Hand hygiene programs should consider skin health. Alcohol-based hand rub can be less irritating than repeated soap-and-water washing when hands are not visibly dirty.
